Questions

How does sign-in work?

Enter your email, receive a 6-digit code, type it in. There are no passwords. If you are new, an account and a private workspace are created for you on the spot.

Does anything go out without approval?

No. The project's skills ask for an explicit yes before sending, publishing or spending, and its autopilot only proposes changes by default.

What can each role do?

Owners manage everything. Admins invite and manage members and viewers. Members create and edit briefs. Viewers can read.

What does it cost?

The agent pack is free under the MIT licence. You pay only for your own AI model usage and any ad or SEO tools you connect with your own keys.
